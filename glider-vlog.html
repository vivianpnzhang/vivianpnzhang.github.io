A long weekend of gliding

a vlog from flying with the Air Cadet gliding program at CFB Trenton and Mountain View

the weekend

I spent a long weekend flying with the Air Cadet gliding program, splitting my time between CFB Trenton and Mountain View. There's nothing quite like it: no engine, just the tow up, the release, and then the quiet up there with only the wind and the instruments telling you what's happening.

Between the early briefings, the waiting on the flight line, and the time in the cockpit, it was a weekend full of learning and a lot of fun. I brought my camera along to show what a few days of flying actually looks like, from the ground crew work to the views from up there.

If you've ever wondered what gliding with cadets is like, I hope this gives you a little taste of it.
