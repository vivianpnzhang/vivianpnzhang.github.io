Music

a few performances, and a bit about the instruments i play

Music has been part of my life for as long as I can remember. Over the years I've been lucky enough to learn five instruments:

  • piano
  • violin
  • clarinet
  • baritone
  • tuba

It definitely didn't happen all at once. Each new instrument started with curiosity and a lot of very questionable first practice sessions. Going from keys to strings to woodwinds to low brass taught me that every instrument asks you to listen a little differently, and I'm still learning something new every time I pick one up.

I'm really grateful to the teachers, conductors and bandmates who were patient with me along the way. Music is where I unwind, how I've met some of my favourite people, and a reminder that getting better at anything mostly comes down to showing up and practicing.
